A Brooklyn animation studio built around drawing.

The studio is led by Scott Berglund, a creative director, animator, illustrator and motion designer with 20 years of experience. Scott studied animation at the Minneapolis College of Art and Design. His drawing has a sketchy, hand-drawn quality that gives every project a human touch.

Berglund stays small on purpose. You work directly with the person drawing your project, and we bring in trusted collaborators when a job calls for more hands.
